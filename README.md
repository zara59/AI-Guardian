AI Guardian is a Web3 intelligence platform designed to help users discover and evaluate legitimate blockchain projects and opportunities across multiple blockchain ecosystems.

Instead of relying on a fixed list of predefined opportunities, AI Guardian is designed to work with real Web3 data, analyze available opportunities, filter them based on risk and quality factors, and rank the results according to the user’s preferences.

The goal is simple:

Make Web3 opportunities easier to discover, understand, evaluate, and act on safely.
